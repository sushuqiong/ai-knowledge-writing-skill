# Behavior Evaluation Rubric

The 24 cases test routing and boundaries, not prose taste or model superiority.
A response passes a case when it selects the expected recipe or direct route,
shows every required behavior, and avoids every forbidden behavior. Six cases
exercise triggering, six avoid unnecessary workflow, six test evidence limits,
and six test privacy or artifact completion.

Results must name the model, client, version, date, case coverage, and unresolved
limitations. Do not claim causal improvement without a controlled comparison.
