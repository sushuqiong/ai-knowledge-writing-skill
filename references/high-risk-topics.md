# High-Risk Topic Guardrails

Use these rules for medicine, public health, law, finance, safety, and other
consequential subjects.

- Identify the population, setting, date, and jurisdiction that bound a claim.
- Prefer current authoritative guidance and primary evidence.
- Separate screening, diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, and research findings.
- Distinguish association from causation and surrogate outcomes from patient
  benefit.
- State when evidence is preliminary, observational, indirect, or conflicting.
- Do not recommend an individual action that requires a licensed professional's
  assessment.
- Add a concise educational-use boundary; do not use a disclaimer to excuse
  unsupported claims.
