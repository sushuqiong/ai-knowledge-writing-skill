# Evidence and Fact Boundaries

## Evidence states

- **Supported**: directly backed by an accessible, relevant source.
- **Inferred**: a reasonable interpretation that is not directly stated.
- **Uncertain**: evidence is incomplete, conflicting, inaccessible, or stale.
- **Opinion**: a value judgment or recommendation, clearly attributed.

Never convert failed access, an empty search, or a missing detail into a negative
fact. Record source scope and date when a claim can change.

## Source priority

1. official documents, regulators, primary research, and first-party repositories;
2. professional organizations, public institutions, and maintained technical docs;
3. reputable synthesis with transparent sourcing;
4. secondary tutorials and social posts only as discovery aids.

For a comparison, apply the same evidence standard to every item. When exact
pricing or timing is unavailable, say so instead of estimating.
