# Recipe: Source or Image to Article

Use when an attachment, screenshot, document, figure, or passage is the starting
point.

1. Inventory what is directly visible or stated.
2. Separate interpretation and missing context.
3. Verify consequential or changing claims independently.
4. Build a new outline based on the reader's question.
5. Rewrite from concepts, not from the source sentence by sentence.
6. Exclude logos, private interface details, filenames, and hidden context unless
   the user explicitly authorizes their use.
