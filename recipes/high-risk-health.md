# Recipe: Medical and High-Stakes Topics

Use the seven-step workflow plus
[high-risk-topics.md](../references/high-risk-topics.md).

Lead with the decision context, not a sensational conclusion. Explain who a
finding applies to, what evidence supports it, what remains uncertain, and which
questions require a qualified professional. Prefer calibrated language only
when the evidence warrants it. Do not promise outcomes or replace individualized
assessment.
