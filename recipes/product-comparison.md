# Recipe: Product or Tool Comparison

Use a dated snapshot and the same comparison dimensions for every product:
owner, first public record, product form, execution location, strengths,
limitations, cost model, permissions, interoperability, and best-fit scenario.

Verify dates, prices, and current capabilities with official sources. Separate
free software from model, cloud, or hosting costs. Do not rank products as
universally best; recommend by scenario and state that plans and quotas change.
