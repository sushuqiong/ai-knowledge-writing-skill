# Recipe: Concept Explainer

Use when the user asks what a concept is, why it matters, how it is used, and
what result to expect.

1. Define it in one plain sentence.
2. Add one bounded analogy.
3. Explain why it became useful or popular.
4. Describe the minimum practical workflow.
5. Name typical outputs and common misunderstandings.
6. End with a one-sentence distinction or takeaway.

Do not turn the article into a methods review or remove a scientific limitation
that changes the meaning.
